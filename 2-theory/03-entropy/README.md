# Entropy and the Thermodynamic Arrow (belt)

Claims: [ACC-001](../../traceability/claims/ACC-001.yaml),
[OPN-005](../../traceability/claims/OPN-005.yaml),
[OPN-008](../../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml). ROADMAP item 5.

## Status

[ACC-001](../../traceability/claims/ACC-001.yaml) — that the low-entropy initial state is
expected rather than anomalous — is the programme's most *comfortable* accommodation and
forbids nothing. The rival treats the low-entropy past as a genuine open problem (Penrose,
Carroll-Chen, inflationary measure arguments), so accommodating it is cheap and does not
by itself distinguish the programmes.

## Where the content would come from

Promotion requires a **quantitative** consequence of "specified order" that
continuous-history cosmology does not permit. Candidate directions, none yet developed:

- A stated entropy *budget* at the actualization boundary, from which a present-epoch
  quantity follows and can be compared against measurement.
- A constraint on the initial Weyl curvature that differs from the rival's expectation.
- A relation between the specified initial order and the observed arrow's rate.

Each is a real research problem. Until one produces a number, ACC-001 stays an
accommodation and the [ledger](../../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md)
counts it as contributing nothing.

## The TRT coupling

[OPN-008](../../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml) closed 2026-08-03 as *common ontology,
divergent belts*, which settles the **ontological** question and leaves the **entropy**
question open and sharper. Position paper §7 states that entropy measures the growth of
accessible states from an intentionally specified beginning; TRT's belt cosmology has
actualization *continuing* to accumulate over cosmic time. Since the core underdetermines
the temporal profile ([EXT-003](../../traceability/claims/EXT-003.yaml)), these are two
legitimate but genuinely different entropy pictures — so CAC owes an account of its own
here rather than an appeal to TRT's. This is the directory where that account gets
written.
