# Actualization (belt)

What discrete cosmological actualization *is*, mathematically. Claims:
[PHY-001](../../traceability/claims/PHY-001.yaml),
[OPN-001](../../traceability/claims/OPN-001.yaml). ROADMAP item 1.

## The question

[PHY-001](../../traceability/claims/PHY-001.yaml) asserts that the Day Four event
involved a rapid actualization of cosmic spacetime into functionally mature extent. It is
the load-bearing physical hypothesis of the programme and it is currently an assertion.

A postulate set must say, at minimum:

1. **What is specified** at the boundary — a metric, a matter distribution, a causal
   structure, a field configuration, or some combination.
2. **Over what** it is specified — the manifold, its topology, its extent.
3. **How the specification connects** to subsequent evolution under
   [CRE-005](../../traceability/claims/CRE-005.yaml). This is the joint at which a
   discrete act must hand off to continuous law without either side being incoherent.
4. **What is *not* specified**, and therefore what ordinary law is left to produce. This
   is the most important of the four and the easiest to skip. Every additional thing
   specified at the boundary is a withdrawal from the programme's falsifiable content —
   see the degeneration watch-point at
   [OPN-006](../../traceability/claims/OPN-006.yaml).
