# Exegesis (belt)

Readings of Genesis 1 that support the [hard core](../../1-hypothesis/hard-core.md)
without being part of it. Claims: [HER-001](../../traceability/claims/HER-001.yaml),
[HER-002](../../traceability/claims/HER-002.yaml),
[HER-003](../../traceability/claims/HER-003.yaml).

## Why exegesis is belt, not core

[CRE-001](../../traceability/claims/CRE-001.yaml) says Genesis records genuine history.
It does **not** say which historical reading is correct. A revised reading of Day Four
leaves CRE-001 standing; treating a preferred reading as though it inherited the core's
immunity is the belt-promotion failure the
[methodology](../../0-program-methods/METHODOLOGY.md) names, and it is the failure this
programme is most likely to commit because the readings feel as settled as the axioms.

## Standing work

- **Day Four as functional appointment**
  ([HER-002](../../traceability/claims/HER-002.yaml)). Needs the competing readings
  stated and priced: framework, analogical-day, functional-ontology. One complication to
  handle explicitly rather than around — the functional reading is also deployed by
  interpreters who reject CRE-001. Borrowing its machinery while rejecting its usual
  conclusion is a position that has to be argued for.
- **Earth-local frame** ([HER-001](../../traceability/claims/HER-001.yaml)). The claim is
  about *narrative viewpoint*. Every downstream physical use must be audited for the
  slide into *preferred relativistic reference frame*, which is a much stronger claim
  that general relativity does not supply freely. This is currently the weakest joint
  between the exegesis and the physics.
- **Sequential interval** ([HER-003](../../traceability/claims/HER-003.yaml)). Needed to
  give "discrete actualization during Creation Week" temporal definiteness.
