## The most exposed accommodation

[ACC-005](../../traceability/claims/ACC-005.yaml) asks whether some dark-sector
phenomena are artifacts of reconstructing a continuous history. The paper correctly calls
this "a hypothesis requiring mathematical development," and the development has a specific
shape it must take.

**Dark matter is constrained by lines that are not historical reconstructions.** Galactic
rotation curves, cluster velocity dispersions, strong and weak lensing, and above all the
Bullet Cluster mass-light separation are *present-epoch* observations. A history-artifact
account has to address them directly; it cannot be satisfied by disputing the timeline.
Until it does, ACC-005 should not be cited as support.

## The CMB is the rival's strongest ground

[PHY-004](../../traceability/claims/PHY-004.yaml) /
[OPN-005](../../traceability/claims/OPN-005.yaml). The acoustic peak structure is a
quantitative fit to a specific pre-recombination plasma history with several independent
parameters, and it encodes a **process**, not a configuration. That couples it tightly to
[OPN-010](../../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) and to
[CRE-007](../../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml). Silence here is the single largest gap
between CAC and a competitive cosmological model.

## The degeneration watch-point

[OPN-006](../../traceability/claims/OPN-006.yaml) — large-scale structure. If observed
structure must be *specified* at creation rather than *grown* under ordinary law, the
maturity principle absorbs an increasing share of the explanatory work and the programme's
content declines toward "it was made that way." Ask on every result whether ordinary law
from the actualized configuration could have produced it instead. If it could have, the
maturity invocation was a withdrawal from falsifiable content.

Note the flip side, which is the opportunity: if actualized initial conditions plus
ordinary gravitational law over a short interval produce a *different* growth history than
the rival, then $f\sigma_8$ from redshift-space distortions and cluster abundance
evolution discriminate — the cleanest form a discriminator can take, because both
programmes then make quantitatively comparable statements about the same measured
quantity.
