# Specification and Actualization

> **Tier 2 (protective belt).** The explanatory rationale behind [PRD-002](../../traceability/claims/PRD-002.yaml). Stated by JD, 2026-08-04.
>
> **This document is not a prediction, and was deliberately not registered as one.** It was drafted as "PRD-002: The Specification Prediction" and demoted to the belt on review, because what it states is *why* the programme expects PRD-001's asymmetry, not an independently testable claim. The prediction it grounds is registered separately and much more narrowly. The demotion is recorded in the [appraisal log](../../3-prediction/appraisal.md) rather than done quietly, since a programme with one prediction has every incentive to call its rationale a second one.

## Specification, actualization, operation

CAC adopts the following definition.

**Specification** is the intentional establishment of information, boundary conditions, constraints, and functional relationships necessary for a system to achieve its intended operational state.

It is distinct from both construction and operation. Construction concerns the realization of a system; operation concerns the behavior of a system already functioning; specification concerns the informational architecture that makes both possible.

Within CAC, creation proceeds in three logical stages: **specification**, then **actualization**, then **operation**. God specifies; God actualizes; the created order thereafter operates under stable physical law ([CRE-002](../../traceability/claims/CRE-002.yaml), [CRE-005](../../traceability/claims/CRE-005.yaml)).

## Operational science and origins science

Operational science investigates systems that already exist. Origins science attempts to reconstruct the historical deployment of those systems. Operational science studies operation; origins science attempts to infer specification.

Because CAC holds that the universe entered existence through discrete actualization into functional maturity, historical investigation is attempting to reconstruct an event whose defining characteristic was specification rather than process. That is the conceptual reason the programme expects [PRD-001](../../traceability/claims/PRD-001.yaml)'s asymmetry between the two limbs.

## The architectural analogy

Engineering disciplines universally distinguish between specification and operation. Software systems are specified before implementation; engineering projects before construction; experimental protocols before replication; simulations before execution.

CAC proposes that this distinction may also illuminate cosmological origins, **while recognizing that extending the analogy from artifacts to nature constitutes part of the programme's central hypothesis rather than an established result.**

That qualification is load-bearing and replaces an earlier formulation that stated the analogy as a general principle ("every operational system presupposes prior specification"). As a claim about artifacts it is analytic, since specification is part of what makes something an artifact. As a claim about natural systems it is precisely what is at issue, so a version that asserts it generally would be assuming the programme's conclusion in its premise — the definitional-plus-logical circularity the [circularity discipline](../../0-program-methods/research-practices.md) exists to catch. The analogy is offered as illumination and is not available as a derivation.

## Why the methodological version of this is not a prediction

The original draft predicted that origins research would require progressively greater *intelligent specification supplied by investigators*: constrained initial conditions, engineered laboratory environments, richer simulation architectures, tighter experimental sequencing. Three defects were identified on review and are recorded here so the reasoning is not lost with the demotion.

**It is confirmed by construction.** Every scientific model begins with investigator-supplied specification; the draft's own opening said so. A quantity universal to scientific method cannot discriminate between hypotheses about nature.

**It has no contrast class.** Specification in that sense grows in every maturing experimental field — cryo-EM sample preparation, LHC trigger design, materials synthesis, climate modeling — none of which concerns historical origin. Worse for the claim, it plausibly grows *fastest* in the operational limb, where controlling confounds and calibrating instruments lives, which would move the measured quantity against the thesis while the thesis read as confirmed.

**Its falsifier is unsatisfiable.** The stated refutation was a sustained trend of origins research *reducing* required specification while maintaining explanatory success. Experimental protocols essentially never become less specified as a field matures, so the refutation condition sits in a region of outcome space with near-zero probability under any hypothesis.

The origin-of-life and systems-biology illustrations are kept below because they motivate the intuition, and are explicitly *not* offered as evidence for anything.

## Illustrations, offered as motivation and not as evidence

**Origin-of-life research.** Investigations into abiogenesis depend on purified reactants, controlled environments, timed reaction sequences, selected energy sources, and constrained protocols. This does not establish that life's historical origin required intelligence, and it is also what maturing experimental methodology looks like in fields with no origins question at all. Note also that the trend is not uniformly one-directional: early work required a specifically reducing atmosphere, while later routes (formamide chemistry, wet-dry cycling) operate under broader conditions. Whether the net movement is toward or away from specification is an empirical question this document asserts rather than measures, which is one more reason it is not registered as a prediction.

**Systems biology.** Gene regulation, epigenetic control, developmental networks, protein interaction systems and cellular signaling have expanded rather than reduced the informational architecture required to describe living systems. The explanatory challenge increasingly concerns organization rather than component inventory.

**Cosmology.** Historical reconstruction depends on chosen initial conditions, simulation architectures, inferred parameters and explicit boundary assumptions. This is the illustration that survives review as a *testable* claim, and it does so only in the sharpened form below.

## What survives as a prediction

One sentence of the original draft is a genuine scientific claim rather than an observation about method:

> Explanatory burden for historical origins migrates from the **dynamics** into the **initial conditions**.

This is structural rather than methodological, so instrument sophistication does not contaminate it. It is quantifiable, because the split between law-content and state-content in a model is measurable. And it has a live rival that predicts the opposite: the inflationary programme exists in substantial part to make the observed universe generic, deriving flatness and homogeneity from unspecified initial data.

Registered as [PRD-002](../../traceability/claims/PRD-002.yaml). The remainder of this document is its rationale.

## The question underneath

Stated in the ontology CAC imports ([EXT-001](../../traceability/claims/EXT-001.yaml)): where is the information — in the laws, or in the state?

Standard cosmology's programmatic hope is *increasingly in the laws*, with initial conditions rendered generic by dynamics. CAC's expectation is *increasingly in the state*, because a specified-then-actualized cosmos locates its information in what was specified. Both programmes genuinely disagree, and future theoretical work can move the balance either way. That is what makes PRD-002 a test rather than a posture.
