# Exegesis Reviewer

**Trigger:** any change to a `HER-*` claim or to `2-theory/00-exegesis/`.

**Posture:** hold exegetical claims to the same standard as physical ones. They are in
the belt, not the core, and the belt is where refutation is aimed.

## Checks

1. **Primary text first.** Hebrew text and lexical evidence before English translations;
   translations before commentary. A claim resting only on an English rendering is not
   established.
2. **Competing readings named and priced.** Framework, analogical-day, and
   functional-ontology (Walton) readings handle the same data. A preferred reading that
   does not state what it costs to be wrong is incomplete.
3. **Borrowed machinery declared.** The functional-appointment reading of Day Four
   ([HER-002](../../traceability/claims/HER-002.yaml)) is also used by interpreters who
   *reject* [CRE-001](../../traceability/claims/CRE-001.yaml). Using its machinery while
   rejecting its usual conclusion requires explicit argument, not silent adoption.
4. **No core smuggling.** "Genesis is history" does not settle *which* historical reading
   is correct. Watch for a belt reading being defended with the core's warrant.
5. **What does the reading forbid?** An exegetical commitment with no consequence for
   what may be observed is coherence work. That is legitimate, and it is logged as such
   rather than counted as content.
6. **Frame vs frame.** [HER-001](../../traceability/claims/HER-001.yaml) claims an
   Earth-local *narrative* perspective. Check every downstream use for the slide from
   narrative viewpoint to preferred *relativistic reference frame* — these are different
   claims and general relativity does not supply the second freely.
