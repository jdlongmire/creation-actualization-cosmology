# VSOK — Vision · Strategy · Objectives · Key Results

The strategic frame for the programme. VSOK is the Tier-0 structured artifact; it sits *above* the falsifiability ladder ([0-program-methods](.) governs tiers 1–3, it is not on the ladder). The Objectives recast the [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md) as OKRs.

---

## Vision

An alternative cosmological research programme founded on a different historical boundary condition: that the universe was **discretely actualized into functional maturity** during Creation Week, and has operated under stable physical law since. The vision is to move this from a coherent position to a programme that issues at least one observational claim distinguishing it from ΛCDM, and to report honestly whichever way that claim resolves.

## Strategy

**The discriminator is the bottleneck, not the exposition.** The position paper is complete as a position; further philosophical elaboration adds weight without exposure. Section 5's reinterpretations are accommodations of known facts and, on the programme's own [methodology](METHODOLOGY.md), contribute nothing to progressiveness. The strategy is therefore single: **find what CAC forbids.**

That inverts the natural build order. Rather than constructing postulates, then field equations, then redshift models, and arriving at observational predictions with a mature apparatus, the programme goes hunting early for the sharpest place it is exposed — currently [OPN-010](../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml), processes observed in transit — on the reasoning that maximum exposure and maximum discriminating power are the same property. Formalism is then built to answer a question already known to be sharp.

Structurally: a [Lakatosian programme](METHODOLOGY.md) with a hard core immune **by decision and said so**, a protective belt of adjustable exegetical and physical hypotheses, and a traceability layer that mechanically separates accommodation from prediction so the programme cannot mistake one for the other.

## Objectives & Key Results

OKR semantics are **epistemic**: a Key Result is achieved when a question is *resolved* — a confirmation or a recorded refutation both count. Only an undecidable or quietly-abandoned question is a miss.

### O1 — State what CAC must hold about light in transit *(the gating objective)*

- **KR1.1** A written position on [OPN-009](../traceability/claims/OPN-009.yaml) (light travel time) stating what CAC would have to hold, with the cost of each available option priced. *(Confidence a defensible option exists: UNCERTAIN.)*
- **KR1.2** A written position on [OPN-010](../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) (processes observed in transit: $(1+z)$ light-curve time dilation, SN 1987A ring geometry, quasar variability). *(LOW.)*
- **KR1.3** From KR1.1–1.2, extract at least one candidate claim that *forbids* an observation. Crude is acceptable; empty is not.

### O2 — Disposition the TRT relation *(unblocks the ontology import)*

- **KR2.1** Choose among the three dispositions in [`relation-to-trt.md`](../1-hypothesis/relation-to-trt.md) and record the choice with its cost in the appraisal log. *(MEDIUM that a defensible disposition exists; the cheapest, "common ontology, divergent belts," weakens §7's inheritance claim and that weakening must be carried into the paper.)*

### O3 — Mathematical postulates for discrete actualization *(depends on O1)*

- **KR3.1** A postulate set for discrete cosmological actualization, constrained by whatever O1 established the postulates must satisfy. *(LOW.)*
- **KR3.2** Compatibility statement with general relativity where applicable — what is retained, what is suspended, over what interval. *(LOW.)*

### O4 — First discriminating prediction *(depends on O1, O3)*

- **KR4.1** One claim at tier 3 with a stated `falsifies` condition, preregistered before evaluation. *(UNCERTAIN.)*

Until KR4.1 lands, the [appraisal](../3-prediction/appraisal.md) verdict remains **unappraised**, and the programme says so wherever it describes itself.

## Non-objectives

- Displacing ΛCDM as a quantitative model. The position paper disclaims this in its abstract; the programme holds the disclaimer.
- Apologetic persuasion. The exegetical and theological case for the [hard core](../1-hypothesis/hard-core.md) is argued on its own grounds and belongs to [oddXian](https://github.com/jdlongmire/oddxian-apologetics); this repository's job is the belt and the predictions.
- Accumulating further accommodations. Section 5 already carries five. A sixth adds no scientific weight, and the [progressiveness report](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md) will show it adding none.
