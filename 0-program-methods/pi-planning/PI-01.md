# PI-1 — Stand up the programme and find the sharpest question

**Milestone:** `PI-1`. **Planned:** 2026-08-03. **Iteration unit:** one problemshift
(see [github-workflow.md](../github-workflow.md)). Soft 4-iteration calendar; the
counter is problems decided, not weeks elapsed.

## Intent

The programme starts with zero predictions and no defensible position on the two
objections a reader raises first. PI-1 does not try to build cosmology. It establishes
what CAC *must hold*, so that PI-2's postulate work is constrained by a question already
known to be sharp rather than by whatever formalism comes to hand.

## Committed objectives

### O1 — State the position on light in transit *(VSOK O1)*

- **O1a** [OPN-009](../../traceability/claims/OPN-009.yaml) — survey the existing
  young-cosmos treatments of light travel time (relativistic time-dilation cosmologies,
  alternative synchrony conventions, in-transit creation), price each against CAC's
  axioms, and state which CAC adopts or why none serves. **Survey, not invention** —
  reinventing a treatment the literature already refuted is the cheapest available
  mistake.
- **O1b** [OPN-010](../../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) — state CAC's position on
  processes observed in transit, beginning with the $(1+z)$ supernova light-curve time
  dilation, which is the best-measured of them.

*Success:* both positions written, with costs priced, in `2-theory/02-metric/`.
*Failure that still closes the iteration:* a written finding that no available option is
consistent with the axioms. That would be a serious result and is recorded as one.

### O2 — Disposition the TRT relation *(VSOK O2)*

- **O2a** Choose among the three dispositions in
  [`relation-to-trt.md`](../../1-hypothesis/relation-to-trt.md), record the choice and
  its cost in the [appraisal log](../../3-prediction/appraisal.md), and carry the
  consequence into the position paper's §7 for v0.2.

*Success:* [OPN-008](../../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml) closed with a stated
disposition. Note that the cheapest disposition weakens §7, and the weakening must be
carried, not quietly dropped.

### O3 — Argue CRE-007 rather than assert it

- **O3a** [CRE-007](../../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml) — supply the argument that
  operational completeness does not entail deception, specifically under the in-transit
  case where the created state encodes an apparently completed process. This is a
  theological argument with a cosmological trigger and it cannot be deferred, because
  O1b's answer will depend on it.

## Stretch

### O4 — First candidate discriminator *(VSOK O4, critical path)*

- **O4a** From O1b, extract at least one claim that *forbids* an observation, however
  crude. Registered as `role: prediction` with a `falsifies` condition fixed in advance,
  or as an accommodation with a `forbids` field recording exactly how far it got.

**Commit semantics:** committing to O4a means committing to *run the attempt to
resolution*, not to a positive result. An honest "no discriminator found, here is why"
closes it.

## Explicitly not in PI-1

- Mathematical postulates ([OPN-001](../../traceability/claims/OPN-001.yaml)). Deferred
  to PI-2 on purpose: postulates built before knowing what they must explain are the
  expensive way round.
- Field equations, CMB, structure formation, dark sector. All gated on OPN-001.
- Any further accommodation. Five is already more than the programme can cash.

## Roles

Research assistance executes; JD holds the approval gates — the OPN-008 disposition, any
promotion of a claim to `role: prediction`, any change to the appraisal verdict, and any
public circulation of the paper.
