# 2026-08-03 — PI-1 O2a: dispositioning the TRT relation (OPN-008)

## What was attempted

Close [OPN-008](../../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml) by choosing among the three
dispositions priced at stand-up: *common ontology divergent belts*, *CAC supersedes
ADCE*, or *independent programmes*.

## What was found

**The decisive evidence was TRT's own registered refutation, not a comparison of the two
programmes.** ADCE's v2 central hypothesis held that continued irreversible actualization
necessarily changes the geometry of realized spacetime. A red-team pass refuted it and
ADCE §8 now records the refutation in its own words: with $N = \rho_4 V_4$, a static
universe accumulates realized history forever at saturated density with zero metric
evolution, so "the capacity bound plus continued actualization therefore constrain $a(t)$
not at all." Falsification criterion 3 fired and is registered. The reformulated
hypothesis is explicitly weaker — "nothing *forces* it."

Three consequences:

1. **The temporal profile of actualization is a free parameter at TRT's core.** The core
   characterizes $\mathcal{A}$ as a mapping (ACT-001, Lean-verified) and stratifies the
   four tiers. Nothing fixes rate-versus-act. ADCE had to *add* a rate structure at belt
   level, and its attempt to derive a geometric consequence from actualization alone
   failed by its own equations. Registered as
   [EXT-003](../../traceability/claims/EXT-003.yaml).
2. **The conflict is belt-versus-belt, and TRT says so independently.** TRT's own OPN-008
   `risk_if_false` reads: "The TRT hard core is untouched either way; this is a
   belt-level physics-facing conjecture."
3. **Neither belt can supersede the other.** ADCE's severe test failed across the natural
   bridge-law grid in every cell ("one probe, two damaged branches, one scheduled
   execution date"). CAC has zero predictions. A supersession claim from either side
   would be an undeveloped programme's claim against a damaged one, on no evidence.

**Disposition: common ontology, divergent belts.** Chosen on the argument, not on cost —
which matters, because it was also the cheapest of the three and would have been the
suspect choice on that basis alone.

## The cost, which the programme pays

Underdetermination cuts both ways. If TRT's core does not entail continuity, it does not
entail discreteness either. So §7's "CAC naturally derives from TRT" is **wrong**, not
merely unsupported, and the correction to "adopts the ontology of" is required rather than
optional. The programme's strongest-sounding grounding argument is withdrawn by the same
argument that closed the problem in its favour.

Recorded in the appraisal log, the paper changelog, and inline in the §7 programme note,
so a reader of the paper meets the correction where the error is.

## What the import buys instead

[EXT-004](../../traceability/claims/EXT-004.yaml) — TRT's four-tier engine imposes a
**disambiguation obligation**. A distant record is a physical structure, hence actual,
hence $L_3$-admissible. The *process it records* must be separately assigned: in $\chi$ or
not. TRT does not forbid an actual record of a non-actual process; it forbids leaving the
question undeclared. Each answer carries a bill — an actual process owes a history in
which it occurred within the programme's interval; a non-actual one owes the argument that
this is not the deception [CRE-007](../../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml) disclaims.

Now a dependency of [OPN-010](../../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml). This is the point
at which the TRT inheritance stops being vocabulary and starts constraining, and it
narrows the option space at the programme's most exposed problem before PI-1 O1b goes
looking there.
