# 2026-08-03 — The Origins Framework, and the likelihood gap it exposes

## What was attempted

JD challenged the stand-up framing of the apparent-age problem and supplied the five-point
[Origins Framework](../../1-hypothesis/origins-framework.md) of which CAC's axioms are the
cosmological specialization. Land it, and settle what survives of the stand-up analysis.

## What was conceded

**The stand-up framing of [OPN-010](../../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) contained a
real error.** It was written as though a time-dilated supernova light curve *is* an age
that CAC must explain away. It is not. The instrument delivers a light curve; the age is a
posterior computed through auxiliary assumptions, and point 4 of the framework is correct
that the prior is doing much of the work. Treating an inference as a datum is a
theory-ladenness failure and it was mine.

## What survives, in stronger form

**The framework does not dissolve OPN-010 — it answers it,** and an answer is a better
outcome than a dissolution because the programme can now be held to a position.

The answer is [EXT-004](../../traceability/claims/EXT-004.yaml) branch two: the record is
actual, the recorded process is not, and this is not deception because (a) the luminaries'
stated function is signs and seasons rather than chronicle
([HER-002](../../traceability/claims/HER-002.yaml)), and (b) the chronological inference is
supplied by the observer's priors, not asserted by the artifact
([EPI-001](../../traceability/claims/EPI-001.yaml)).

**[CRE-007](../../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml) is upgraded from asserted to argued.**
The stand-up audit flagged that the paper asserted "operational completeness is not
deception" without argument. Point 2 supplies one by generalizing the maturity pattern past
Creation Week — the wine at Cana, healings that skip the normal biological process. The
Cana wine carried chemical properties a chemist would date to years of fermentation and
deceived nobody, because deception requires an intent to induce false belief and a
functional artifact produced for a stated function makes no claim about its own provenance.

Residual, recorded on the claim: the argument is strongest for **configurations**. Records
of *processes* with cross-channel internal consistency — SN 1987A's neutrino burst, optical
rise, and ring light-echo timing, mutually consistent — are the harder case, and the reply
there runs through the functional-purpose argument rather than the Cana analogy directly.

## What the framework's own standard exposes

Point 4 is stated in Bayesian terms, so it should be held to Bayesian standards.

$$P(H \mid E) \propto P(E \mid H)\, P(H)$$

Point 4 correctly identifies that $P(H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$ is set near zero by standard
practice and that this drives the result. But **the recommended prior shift cannot be
computed, because CAC has never specified $P(E \mid H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$.** The programme has
no likelihood function: no statement of what a discretely actualized cosmos should be
expected to look like, in what respects, with what distribution.

Two consequences, registered as [OPN-011](../../traceability/claims/OPN-011.yaml):

- The argument can be *asserted* but not *run*. It neutralizes an objection; it does not
  produce a claim.
- Absent a likelihood, **any** observation is absorbable by "your priors generated that
  inference." That is unfalsifiability in its strongest form, and it is the direct cause of
  the ledger reading zero.

This is not a defect in the framework. Point 4 names the factor standard practice gets
wrong and, in doing so, names exactly the factor CAC has not supplied. A likelihood is what
a research programme is *for* producing.

**OPN-011 supersedes OPN-010 as the sharpest problem in the registry** and is the direct
precondition of [OPN-007](../../traceability/claims/OPN-007.yaml).

## Where the programme gets its teeth — point 5, constructively

Methodological designism's defensive use is obvious: it blocks the charge that CAC licenses
arbitrary miracle-invocation. Its constructive use is more important and was unexploited.

If law is the rule, everything after Creation Week is ordinary physics acting on an
actualized initial configuration. That is substantive, constrained, and computable — and it
requires no relitigation of apparent age, which is where these arguments usually stall. It
is also the one place where $P(E \mid H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$ is *not* hostage to the creation
act itself, since the post-creation era runs on law by CAC's own commitment.

[`discriminators.md`](../../3-prediction/discriminators.md) is reordered accordingly:
EPI-002's constructive limb now leads, processes-in-transit drops to second, and the
ROADMAP's PI-2 is redirected from postulates to the likelihood.

Point 5 also imposes a cost discipline the programme must self-enforce. If intervention is
the exception, invoking it is expensive — and every appeal to actualization to explain what
ordinary law could have produced is a withdrawal from falsifiable content. Under EPI-002
that is a violation of CAC's *own* commitments, not merely a strategic error. This
strengthens the [degeneration watch-point](../../2-theory/open-problems.md) considerably.
