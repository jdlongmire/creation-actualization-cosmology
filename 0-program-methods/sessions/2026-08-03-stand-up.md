# 2026-08-03 — Programme stand-up

## What was attempted

Stand up Creation Actualization Cosmology as a research programme repository from JD's
position paper, on the Lakatosian ladder used by
[TRT](https://github.com/jdlongmire/triadic-reality-theory).

## What was found

**CAC warrants its own programme, not a sub-project under TRT.** A Lakatosian programme is
individuated by its hard core, and CAC's core (historical Genesis, discrete actualization,
functional maturity, stable post-creation law) is distinct from TRT's (χ ≡ A(I∞ | L₃) and
the co-requiring primitives). LRT folds into TRT because it formalizes a *constituent* of
TRT's core; CAC does not.

**The §7 inheritance from TRT is not established.** Reading TRT's belt-level cosmology
note (ADCE) directly rather than the §7 summary: ADCE develops a *continuous* accumulation
of realized causal history over cosmic time, on a causal-set number-volume substrate with
targets anchored to DESI. CAC asserts a *discrete* actualization. These are not obviously
compatible. Registered as [OPN-008](../../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml) with three
dispositions priced in [`relation-to-trt.md`](../../1-hypothesis/relation-to-trt.md); none
chosen. The cheapest disposition weakens §7 from "derives from" to "adopts the ontology
of," and that weakening has to be carried into v0.2 rather than dropped.

**All five §5 reinterpretations are accommodations, not predictions.** Each handles a fact
known before the claim was made, and on Lakatos's criterion contributes nothing to
progressiveness. The paper says as much in §8–§9; what was added here is the *mechanism*
that keeps it said: a `role: accommodation` claim type with required `forbids` and
`rival_account` fields, counted separately from predictions in a generated
[progressiveness report](../../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md), and an
`honest-status` check that fails if the appraisal log stops matching the ledger.

The ledger at stand-up reads: **0 predictions, 5 accommodations, 0 forbidding anything.**

**Two objections the paper does not name were registered.**
[OPN-009](../../traceability/claims/OPN-009.yaml) (light travel time) and
[OPN-010](../../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) (processes observed in transit —
$(1+z)$ supernova light-curve time dilation, SN 1987A ring geometry, quasar variability,
binary pulsar spin-down). OPN-010 is the sharper of the two, is less often addressed, and
bears directly on [CRE-007](../../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml)'s claim that
operational completeness does not entail deception — a claim the paper asserts but does
not argue. It is also flagged as the most promising place to look for the programme's
first discriminator, on the reasoning that maximum exposure and maximum discriminating
power are the same property.

**One wording defect recorded.** §5 uses "CAC predicts" twice (Hubble tension, JWST).
Neither forbids anything; both are compatible with any outcome including none. Recorded
against [ACC-003](../../traceability/claims/ACC-003.yaml) and
[ACC-004](../../traceability/claims/ACC-004.yaml) as a v0.2 correction rather than
silently kept.

## Effect on the appraisal

**None on the verdict — Unappraised, and now precisely so.** Standing the programme up is
housekeeping, not content. What changed is that the programme can now *measure* whether it
has content, and currently measures zero.
