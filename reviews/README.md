# Reviews

Adversarial review is a contribution
([research-practices #9](../0-program-methods/research-practices.md)). Each review lives
in `<date>-<reviewer-or-topic>/` with a `review.md` and a `response.md`.

## Who to ask

Reviews are actively sought from readers who **reject the hard core**. A review from
someone who shares it cannot test the belt independently, and the belt is the part that
has to survive.

The programme should expect, and should welcome, reviews that:

- press [OPN-009](../traceability/claims/OPN-009.yaml) and
  [OPN-010](../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) hard, since they are the first
  objections and the programme has no answer yet;
- point out that §5 accommodates rather than predicts — the programme has already
  recorded this against itself in the
  [appraisal](../3-prediction/appraisal.md) and the
  [progressiveness ledger](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md), and a
  reviewer who finds it independently is confirming the instrument works;
- attack [CRE-007](../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml)'s deception disclaimer, which is
  currently asserted rather than argued.

## What a successful review looks like

One that forces a confidence label down, exposes a circularity, compels a retraction, or
shows that a claimed discriminator does not discriminate. These are recorded as
contributions, with the response, and the appraisal log records the effect.

An empty review directory at the start of a programme is normal. An empty one after the
programme has circulated is a finding about the programme.
