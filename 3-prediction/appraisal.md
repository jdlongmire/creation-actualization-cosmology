# Programme Appraisal (Progressive vs Degenerating)

> The Lakatosian self-audit. A research programme is **progressive** if it predicts novel facts and some corroborate; **degenerating** if it only accommodates facts post hoc. This file is the standing answer to: *which is CAC, right now?*
>
> The mechanical counterpart is the generated [progressiveness report](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md), regenerated on every build.

## Current verdict (CAC v0.1)

**Empirically unappraised. One theoretically progressive step taken, of a weaker species than the programme still owes.**

Superseding the stand-up verdict of *unappraised with zero novel content*, which held until 2026-08-04.

CAC is a research programme in the literal Lakatosian sense: it has an articulated [hard core](../1-hypothesis/hard-core.md), an articulated positive heuristic (the [ROADMAP](../0-program-methods/ROADMAP.md)), and a stated intention to become testable ([position paper §9](../1-hypothesis/paper/CAC-v0.1.md)). It now also has **one prediction** — [PRD-001](../traceability/claims/PRD-001.yaml), the historical complexity asymmetry — stated with a metric, a decision rule and a refutation condition fixed in advance. That is a theoretically progressive step: the programme has stated something that could be wrong.

**Three qualifications hold the verdict at its correct strength, and none of them is optional.**

*It is a programme-level appraisal claim, not a novel-fact prediction.* PRD-001 forbids a pattern in how a rival's models evolve. It does not forbid an observation of the world. Both are legitimate Lakatosian objects and they are not interchangeable, and the ledger's count of "1 prediction" must not be read as "1 novel-fact prediction." [OPN-011](../traceability/claims/OPN-011.yaml) — the missing likelihood function — is untouched by it and remains the sharpest open problem.

*Its protocol is unexecuted.* No baseline has been recorded, and no trajectory is measurable without one. Until the H1–H3 counting conventions are fixed and current values logged, PRD-001 is a stated prediction rather than a running test.

*One of its four components currently points the wrong way.* H4 (reconstruction stability) has arguably moved against CAC across Planck 2013 → 2015 → 2018. This is recorded now rather than discovered later, and if H4 keeps converging the claim loses a quarter of its instrument.

**Nothing has corroborated.** Empirical progressiveness requires a prediction to be *made* and then *survive*. CAC has done the first.

They are generated by [`reciprocity_check.py`](../0-program-methods/ops/reciprocity_check.py) on every build rather than asserted here, and the current reading is unflattering: the programme is adding auxiliary structure considerably faster than it is adding anything that could be wrong. Stand-up is a legitimate excuse for that and it expires.

All five of the position paper's §5 reinterpretations — low initial entropy, redshift, the Hubble tension, JWST early structure, the dark sector — are accommodations of facts that were known before the claims were made. On Lakatos's criterion they contribute **nothing** to progressiveness however coherent they are. This is not a criticism of §5; it is what §5 is. The paper says so itself in §8 and §9, and the [methodology](../0-program-methods/METHODOLOGY.md) makes it structural so it cannot quietly stop being said.

**One wording correction is carried into v0.2.** §5 uses "CAC predicts" twice — in the Hubble tension and JWST paragraphs. Neither is a prediction in the Popperian sense: both are compatible with any observed outcome including none. Per [research-practices #1](../0-program-methods/research-practices.md), the wording is recorded here as a correction rather than silently kept, because "predicts" doing accommodation's work is the exact slippage the programme is most at risk of.

## What would move the needle to *progressive*

One claim that forbids an observation ΛCDM permits, or permits one ΛCDM forbids, stated with its evaluation protocol **in advance** — then corroborated.

| A derived magnitude or sign for the early-late $H_0$ offset | [ACC-003](../traceability/claims/ACC-003.yaml) promotion | OPN-001, OPN-004 |

The most likely source of a real discriminator is [OPN-010](../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) — processes observed in transit. It is where the programme is most exposed, and exposure and discriminating power are the same property seen from opposite ends.

A *theoretically* progressive step (stating something that forbids an observation) precedes an *empirically* progressive one (the observation goes the programme's way). **CAC has taken the first and not the second** — and the step it took, PRD-001, forbids a pattern in a rival's model development rather than an observation of the world. The routes tabled above are still the ones that would supply the stronger kind, and none of them is closed by PRD-001 existing.

## What would mark *degeneration* — to be recorded as plainly as success

- **Accommodation accumulation.** A sixth, seventh, eighth §5-style reinterpretation while the prediction count stays at zero. The [progressiveness report](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md) will show it adding nothing, and that showing is the point.
- **Maturity absorbing the explanatory load.** Every unexplained structure being resolved by "it was created that way." [OPN-006](../traceability/claims/OPN-006.yaml) is the named watch-point: if large-scale structure must be *specified* rather than *grown*, the maturity principle has become unfalsifiable in practice.
- **Belt promotion.** Defending a threatened exegetical or physical hypothesis by treating it as though it inherited the core's immunity. [HER-002](../traceability/claims/HER-002.yaml) and [HER-003](../traceability/claims/HER-003.yaml) are in the belt precisely so this is visible when attempted.
- **Riding a moving literature.** Continuing to cite the strong form of the JWST anomaly, or a Hubble tension that systematics have closed, after the literature has moved. [ACC-004](../traceability/claims/ACC-004.yaml) already carries this warning.
- **Rounding the status up.** Describing the programme as anything beyond what this log's standing verdict says. Since 2026-08-04 that verdict is *empirically unappraised, one theoretically progressive step taken* — so citing PRD-001 as though the programme were progressive, or as though a novel-fact prediction had been made, is the rounding-up this line forbids. `honest_status_check.py` enforces the weaker half mechanically by requiring every novel-content claim to be named here; the stronger half is a discipline.
- **Burden ratio climbing while the prediction count stands still.** CAC's own instrument, per PRD-001's reciprocity clause, reported by [`reciprocity_check.py`](../0-program-methods/ops/reciprocity_check.py) on every build. Stand-up excuses the current 41.0; a year of it would be the programme exhibiting exactly the signature its own thesis attributes to a framework with wrong boundary conditions.
- **Silence on OPN-009 / OPN-010.** A programme that never states its position on light in transit has chosen unfalsifiability by omission.
