## What is imported

CAC imports TRT's central identity as its ontological setting ([EXT-001](../traceability/claims/EXT-001.yaml)):

$$\chi \equiv \mathcal{A}(I \mid L)$$

Logic constrains information; actualization produces physical reality. Position paper §7 draws two consequences: creation is the intentional actualization of coherent informational structure rather than the emergence of order from disorder, and entropy measures the growth of accessible physical states from an intentionally specified beginning.

The import is genuine and load-bearing. Without an ontology in which actualization is a primitive act rather than a limit of natural process, "discrete divine actualization" has no formal home.

## The conflict, as registered at stand-up

TRT's belt-level cosmology note, [ADCE](https://github.com/jdlongmire/triadic-reality-theory/blob/main/2-theory/04-cosmology/adce.md), develops a *continuous* accumulation of realized causal history over cosmic time on a causal-set number-volume substrate. CAC asserts a *discrete* actualization of the observable heavens. The question was whether both can be developments of the same ontology.

## The argument that settles it

The decisive evidence is **TRT's own registered refutation**, not a compromise between the programmes.

ADCE's v2 hypothesis read: *continued irreversible actualization necessarily changes the geometry of realized spacetime.* An independent red-team pass refuted it, and ADCE §8 now records the refutation in its own words:

> With $N = \rho_4 V_4$ and $V_4 = V_c \int_0^t a^3\, dt'$, $\dot N = \rho_4 V_c a^3 > 0$ for any $a(t) > 0$. A static universe accumulates 4-volume, hence realized history, linearly and forever at saturated density with zero metric evolution... **The capacity bound plus continued actualization therefore constrain $a(t)$ not at all.**

ADCE's reformulated hypothesis is correspondingly weaker and, in its own words, "nothing *forces* it": expansion registers an *accelerating* actualization rate, which is an additional dynamical posit that the ontology does not supply.

Three things follow, and they settle the question.

**1. The temporal profile of $\mathcal{A}$ is a free parameter at TRT's core.** TRT's hard core characterizes $\mathcal{A}$ as a *mapping*: [ACT-001](https://github.com/jdlongmire/triadic-reality-theory/blob/main/traceability/claims/ACT-001.yaml) says actualizing action maps admissible structure to actual structure, and every actual structure is $L_3$-admissible. The four-tier engine stratifies representable ⊃ admissible ⊃ actual ⊃ outcome-actual. **Nothing in the core fixes whether $\mathcal{A}$ operates at a rate or in an act, continuously or discretely.** ADCE had to *add* a rate structure at belt level, and when it tried to derive a geometric consequence from actualization alone, the derivation failed by its own equations. Registered as [EXT-003](../traceability/claims/EXT-003.yaml).

**2. The conflict is belt-versus-belt, and TRT says so.** TRT's own OPN-008 states the point directly: *"The TRT hard core is untouched either way; this is a belt-level physics-facing conjecture."* CAC's discreteness posit and ADCE's rate posit are competing belt developments choosing differently on a parameter the core leaves open. Neither threatens the shared core, and neither refutes the other at the level of ontology.

**3. Neither belt is in a position to supersede the other.** ADCE's status by TRT's own appraisal: Target 3 closed negative, the severe test failed across the natural bridge-law grid in every cell, and the honest cycle-close is "one probe, two damaged branches, one scheduled execution date." CAC's status by its own appraisal: zero predictions. A supersession claim from either side would be a claim made by an undeveloped programme against a damaged one, on no evidence.

## The disposition

**Compatible cores, composable belts.** CAC and ADCE are distinct belt developments of a shared hard core, differing on the temporal profile of $\mathcal{A}$ — a parameter the core genuinely leaves free, as TRT's own refutation of the forcing form demonstrates.

This is stronger than "the programmes happen to be compatible." It identifies *where* they differ, *why* the difference is legitimate, and *what* would decide between them: not an argument about the ontology, which underdetermines both, but the observational performance of each belt.

### The cores share no proposition on which they could disagree

Read the two hard cores side by side and the compatibility is categorical rather than lucky.

TRT's core constrains the **form and status of the actual**: the central identity, the three co-requiring primitives, the four-tier engine, and the posit that actuality is a genuine status distinct from possibility. It fixes no content, no occasion, and no duration for any particular actualization.

CAC's core makes **historical and content claims about one actualization event**: Genesis as history, actualization rather than culmination, functional maturity, Day Four, stable law thereafter.

Every CAC commitment is a claim TRT's core leaves open, and no TRT commitment is one CAC denies. The one place they could have collided is duration, and §8's static-universe result closes it: the temporal profile is not fixed by the core.

**TRT's core is therefore silent on duration.** Where a timeline appears on the TRT side it belongs to ADCE's *belt*, which develops its bridge laws inside the standard expansion history — matter-era deceleration until $z \approx 0.7$, DESI DR2 BAO fits, a phantom crossing near $z \approx 0.4$. "ADCE works within the conventional timeline" is accurate. "TRT requires deep time" is not, and CAC's disposition depends on that distinction holding.

Two things the compatibility does **not** license, both easy to slide into:

- **Compatibility is permission, not derivation.** The underdetermination argument that establishes it is the same one that killed §7's "naturally derives from." Keeping only the favourable half is the conventionalist twist applied in CAC's own favour.
- **The cores are not equally warranted.** TRT claims immunity by *necessity* — the denial of $L_3$ is self-refuting. CAC claims immunity by *methodological decision* and says so on the page. CAC cannot inherit TRT's warrant by adopting its ontology.

### The belts compose, they do not merely coexist

The 2026-08-03 wording said *divergent belts*. That was right about the free parameter and wrong about the consequence.

ADCE v3.2 places all dynamical load on the bridge law (§9.4), and its candidate laws are **rate-shaped rather than stock-shaped**. Proportional growth $\ddot N = \lambda \dot N$ gives $\dot N \propto e^{\lambda t}$ and the attractor $H^* = \lambda/3$ *independently of any initial stock* $N_0$. Horizon-coupled growth ties the rate to horizon area, a geometric quantity likewise independent of the absolute count. A functionally mature initial actualized stock is invisible to both.

So CAC's act and ADCE's rate are answers to **different questions** — the initial condition and the growth law — and a mature stock plus a growth law is a coherent joint position that neither belt has stated. Registered as [EXT-005](../traceability/claims/EXT-005.yaml). The warrant is a reading of ADCE's own stated laws, not a re-run of its bridge-law grid scan, and the claim is held at that strength.

### Where composition is not free — the one stock-shaped quantity

ADCE's declared rival supplies the exception. Everpresent $\Lambda \sim \pm\hbar/\sqrt{V_4}$ (Sorkin 1997; Ahmed, Dodelson, Greene and Sorkin 2004) depends on the total 4-volume rather than on its rate of growth, and with $V_4 \sim (ct)^4$ this gives $\Lambda \propto t^{-2}$. Substituting a realized 4-volume of order $10^4$ yr for one of order $10^{10}$ yr raises $\Lambda$ by roughly **thirteen orders of magnitude** against the observed value.

Two consequences, registered as [OPN-012](../traceability/claims/OPN-012.yaml):

- If CAC adopts causal-set kinematics at all, it must actualize $V_4$ **itself** mature, not merely its contents. That is [EXT-004](../traceability/claims/EXT-004.yaml) branch two in its most committing form, and it owes [CRE-007](../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml) the corresponding argument.
- The fluctuation $\delta N \sim \sqrt{N}$ is a statistic *of a birth process*. A stock produced in a single act does not obviously carry the same spectrum as one grown over $10^{10}$ yr. If that survives being made precise, act-versus-accumulation has an observational signature — which would be the first thing the programme has produced that is shaped like $P(E \mid H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$ rather than like a reply to an objection.

Confidence on the second limb is **LOW** and the causal-set literature check has not been done. It is registered as a lead, not a result.

## The cost, stated plainly

Underdetermination cuts both ways, and CAC pays for the argument it just used.

**§7's inheritance claim is wrong and must be corrected.** If TRT's core does not entail continuity, it equally does not entail discreteness. CAC does not "naturally derive from" TRT. It *adopts* TRT's ontology and *adds* a discreteness posit that the ontology permits but does not supply. The paper's strongest-sounding grounding argument is not available, and pretending otherwise after establishing the underdetermination would be the conventionalist twist applied to the programme's own favour.

What the import does supply is real but narrower: a formal setting in which actualization is a primitive act rather than a limit of process, so that "discrete divine actualization" names something the ontology has a category for. That is a home, not an argument.

## What the import buys — a constraint, not decoration

The inheritance earns its keep by *constraining* CAC where CAC is weakest.

TRT's four-tier engine forces a disambiguation that a looser ontology would let CAC avoid. Every distant record — a supernova light curve, the SN 1987A ring geometry, a pulsar's spin-down — is a physical structure, hence actual, hence (by [ACT-001](https://github.com/jdlongmire/triadic-reality-theory/blob/main/traceability/claims/ACT-001.yaml), Lean-verified) $L_3$-admissible. But the *process the record records* must be separately assigned: is it in $\chi$ or is it not?

TRT does not forbid an actual record of a non-actual process, any more than an actual photograph requires an actual unicorn. What it forbids is leaving the question open. An in-transit account must **declare**, for each class of distant record, whether the recorded process is actual — and each answer carries a different bill:

| Declaration | What it then owes |
|---|---|
| The process is actual | A history in which it occurred, within the programme's interval. This is [OPN-009](../traceability/claims/OPN-009.yaml)/[OPN-010](../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml) in their hardest form. |
| The process is not actual | An account of why an actual record of a non-actual process is not the deception [CRE-007](../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml) disclaims — and the disclaimer is currently asserted, not argued. |

Registered as [EXT-004](../traceability/claims/EXT-004.yaml) and carried as a dependency of [OPN-010](../traceability/claims/OPN-010.yaml). This converts "CAC should say something about light in transit" into "CAC must assign a tier and declare a status," which is the difference between a gesture and a position.

## Residual — a finding owed back to TRT

The free-parameter result is not recorded on the TRT side. TRT's hard core does not name the temporal profile of $\mathcal{A}$ as an open choice, and ADCE's §8 refutation establishes that it is one without generalizing the point beyond cosmology.

That is a contribution CAC can make upstream: **the temporal profile of $\mathcal{A}$ — rate-like or act-like — is an undetermined parameter of TRT's core, and belt developments must declare their choice.** Offering it to TRT is JD's call, as is whether it lands as a TRT open-problem claim or a note in its hard core.
