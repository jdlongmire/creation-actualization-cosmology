# Relation to Triadic Reality Theory

> **Status:** the inheritance is asserted at the level of ontology and is **unresolved at the level of cosmology**. Tracked as [OPN-008](../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml). This file states what is imported, what is not, and what the open conflict is.

## What is imported

CAC imports TRT's central identity as its ontological setting ([EXT-001](../traceability/claims/EXT-001.yaml)):

$$\chi \equiv \mathcal{A}(I \mid L)$$

Logic constrains information; actualization produces physical reality. Position paper §7 draws two consequences:

1. Creation is not the emergence of order from disorder but the **intentional actualization of coherent informational structure**. The order of the universe reflects rational specification rather than statistical accident.
2. Entropy measures the subsequent growth of accessible physical states **from an intentionally specified beginning**, which is the setting in which the low-entropy boundary condition ([ACC-001](../traceability/claims/ACC-001.yaml)) is expected rather than anomalous.

The import is genuine and load-bearing: without an ontology in which actualization is a primitive act rather than a limit of natural process, "discrete divine actualization" has no formal home. TRT supplies that home.

## What is *not* imported

TRT's protective belt. In particular, TRT's belt-level cosmology note — [Actualization-Driven Cosmological Expansion (ADCE)](https://github.com/jdlongmire/triadic-reality-theory/blob/main/2-theory/04-cosmology/adce.md) — is not imported, and cannot be imported as it stands.

## The open conflict (OPN-008)

ADCE develops the hypothesis that cosmic expansion is the geometric registration of an **accelerating rate of irreversible actualization**, with realized causal history accumulating continuously over cosmic time. Its kinematic substrate is the causal-set number-volume correspondence; its observational targets are anchored to DESI DR2, over a standard expansion history.

That is a **continuous-accumulation** cosmology. CAC's [CRE-002](../traceability/claims/CRE-002.yaml) and [CRE-004](../traceability/claims/CRE-004.yaml) assert a **discrete** actualization of the observable heavens. The two are not obviously reconcilable, and the position paper's phrase "naturally derives from TRT" should not be read as claiming they are.

Three dispositions are available, and the programme has chosen none of them yet:

| Disposition | What it would require | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Common-ontology, divergent belts** | Show that both are admissible belt developments of the same core, differing on a genuinely free parameter (whether $\mathcal{A}$ acts at a rate or in an act) | Cheapest; but it concedes that TRT's ontology *underdetermines* cosmology, which weakens the paper §7 inheritance claim to "compatible with" rather than "derives from" |
| **CAC supersedes ADCE** | Argue that discrete actualization is the correct reading of $\mathcal{A}$ and that ADCE's continuous rate is a derived appearance | Strongest claim; requires CAC to explain everything ADCE addresses, including late-time acceleration, which it currently does not |
| **Independent programmes** | Drop the §7 inheritance to an acknowledgement of shared vocabulary | Honest and cheap, but gives up the ontological grounding that makes "actualization" more than a word |

**Until this is settled, the correct statement of the relation is "CAC adopts TRT's ontology and is not yet shown to be consistent with TRT's cosmological belt."** Any stronger statement in a public-circulation copy of the paper would be an ad hoc smoothing of a known conflict, which the [methodology](../0-program-methods/METHODOLOGY.md) classes as degenerating.

## Why this is registered rather than resolved

The conflict was found at programme stand-up by reading ADCE directly rather than relying on the summary in §7. Registering a known unresolved conflict as an open problem, with its resolution options priced, is a progressive move. Discovering it later, after belt work has been built on the assumption of compatibility, would be expensive.
