## The seven points

1. **The Bible is the material authoritative source of truth.**

2. **God creates or produces systems functionally mature** — Adam and Eve, the wine at Cana, miraculous healings that skip the normal biological processes.

3. **Given 1 and 2, it is reasonable to reject the hypothesis of deep time as necessary.**

4. **Systems that appear "old" are the result of a set of naturalistic Bayesian priors that do not take into account 1 and 2.**

5. **The universe is contingent and necessarily requires intervention by a non-contingent, logical, informational, and actualizable source — the Logos.**

6. **Special intervention is by definition the exception and not the rule.** Methodological designism, which is how science began, is therefore a reasonable normative operational mode.

7. **If the universe were uniformly explainable by naturalism, there would not be so many surprises.** The set is listed below.

## Three things the framework does that the position paper did not

**It generalizes the maturity pattern beyond creation.** The paper's instances are Adam and the fruit trees, both inside Creation Week, which leaves maturity looking like a special feature of the creation event. Point 2's further instances — the wine at Cana, healings that skip the normal biological process — are *post*-creation acts, and they establish that functional maturity is characteristic of divine action as such.

This matters directly for [CRE-007](../traceability/claims/CRE-007.yaml). The stand-up audit recorded that the paper's "operational completeness is not deception" was **asserted rather than argued**. Point 2 supplies the argument: the Cana wine had chemical properties a chemist would date to years of fermentation, and nobody at the wedding was deceived, because the artifact's purpose was never to testify to its own history. Deception requires an intent to induce false belief, and a functional artifact produced for a stated function makes no claim about its provenance. The charge does not attach.

**It states the modal claim correctly.** Point 3 rejects deep time as *necessary*, not as false. That is a weaker and much more defensible claim than the position paper implies, and the programme should hold it at that strength. What is denied is the entailment from the observational corpus to deep time; what is not asserted is that the corpus is inconsistent with it.

**It states the inference problem in the right currency.** Point 4 is the sharpest of the seven. "This system appears old" is not an observation; it is a posterior, computed under a likelihood and a prior. The prior in standard practice assigns effectively zero weight to discrete actualization, so the posterior concentrates on deep time regardless of what the data are. Change the prior and the posterior moves. This is straightforwardly correct as an epistemology of the inference, and it is the correct reply to a large class of "but it *looks* old" objections.

## The reservation the programme must carry — there is no likelihood function

Point 4's argument is stated in Bayesian terms, so hold it to Bayesian standards.

$$P(H \mid E) \propto P(E \mid H)\, P(H)$$

Point 4 correctly observes that $P(H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$ is set near zero by standard practice, and that this drives the result. But the shift it recommends cannot actually be *computed*, because **CAC has not specified $P(E \mid H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$.** The programme currently has no likelihood function: no statement of what a discretely actualized cosmos should be expected to look like, in what respects, with what distribution.

Two consequences follow, and they are the programme's central problem rather than a side issue.

**The argument cannot be run, only asserted.** Without a likelihood, "the appearance of age is prior-dependent" is a correct observation about *someone else's* inference that does not yet license any inference of its own. It neutralizes an objection; it does not produce a claim.

**Without a likelihood, nothing is forbidden.** Any observation whatever can be absorbed by "your priors generated that inference." That is the unfalsifiability charge in its strongest form, and it is exactly why the [progressiveness ledger](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md) reads zero. The absence is registered as [OPN-011](../traceability/claims/OPN-011.yaml), which is now the programme's sharpest open problem and the direct precondition of [OPN-007](../traceability/claims/OPN-007.yaml).

The framework is not damaged by this. A likelihood is exactly the kind of thing a research programme is *for* producing, and point 4 identifies precisely where to build it. But until it exists, point 4 is a defensive result, and the programme should describe it as one.

## Point 5 — what the contingency ground delivers, and what it does not

Point 5 supplies the metaphysical ground of the creative act. [CRE-002](../traceability/claims/CRE-002.yaml) asserts actualization rather than culmination; point 5 gives the reason that is the *required* category rather than the preferred one, since a contingent order does not account for its own existence and requires a source that is not itself contingent.

The four attributes it names — non-contingent, logical, informational, actualizable — are recognisably [TRT's](relation-to-trt.md) primitives in theological register. **That convergence is stated in prose and is deliberately not drawn as a dependency edge in the registry.** Drawing one would re-assert the "naturally derives from TRT" claim that [OPN-008](../traceability/claims/OPN-008.yaml) withdrew, through the front door this time. The convergence is real and worth noticing; it is a derivation in neither direction.

What point 5 does *not* deliver matters equally. A contingency argument establishes that there is a non-contingent source. It says nothing about the temporal profile of what that source does, so it supplies neither discreteness, nor functional maturity, nor any timeline. Those are CRE-002, CRE-003 and CRE-008, argued on separate grounds. Reading more out of point 5 than this is the same over-reading that [CRE-008](../traceability/claims/CRE-008.yaml)'s notes warn against for point 3.

## Point 6 is where the programme gets its teeth

Methodological designism holds that special intervention is the exception and law is the norm. Its defensive use is obvious — it blocks the charge that CAC licenses arbitrary miracle-invocation. Its *constructive* use is more important and is currently unexploited.

If law is the norm, then everything after Creation Week is ordinary physics operating on an actualized initial configuration. That is a substantive, constrained, and **falsifiable** claim, and it does not depend on relitigating the appearance of age. Ordinary law acting on a specified initial state over a short interval either does or does not reproduce what is observed, and the answer is computable in principle.

This is where the discriminator hunt should concentrate. See [`../3-prediction/discriminators.md`](../3-prediction/discriminators.md) and [OPN-006](../traceability/claims/OPN-006.yaml).

Point 6 also carries a discipline the programme must self-impose: **if intervention is the exception, then invoking it is costly.** Every appeal to actualization to explain a structure that ordinary law could have produced is a withdrawal from the programme's falsifiable content, and point 6 is the reason that is a violation of CAC's own commitments rather than merely a strategic error. This is the [degeneration watch-point](../2-theory/open-problems.md).

The parenthetical that methodological designism is "how science began" is a historical observation about the motivations of early modern natural philosophy. It is defensible and it is not load-bearing: the warrant for point 6 is the argument above, not the genealogy. The programme should not lean on the genealogy, because a genetic claim about why a method was adopted establishes nothing about whether the method is correct.

### The branch point — and which one CAC actually contests

The genealogy does one piece of real work, though, and it is not the one it looks like. Naming *where* natural science branched from design-assuming natural philosophy fixes the scope of the disagreement, and the branch was **two transitions rather than one**. Registered as [EPI-003](../traceability/claims/EPI-003.yaml).

**First: final causes exit proximate explanation.** Bacon's *Novum Organum* (1620) separates final from efficient causes; Descartes' *Principia Philosophiae* (1644) bars final causes from physics. From there, method excludes teleological explanation from the account of how a system operates.

**CAC does not contest this, and should say so loudly.** Point 6 is compatible with it by construction — conduct inquiry expecting stable law while holding the metaphysics open. A programme that appears to be reopening *this* transition is arguing for teleology inside proximate physics, which is the "God of the gaps" position CAC disclaims, and a reader who cannot tell the two apart will assume the worse one.

**Second: uniformitarian historical inference.** Hutton's *Theory of the Earth* (1788) and Lyell's *Principles of Geology* (1830–33) establish that the past is explained by causes now in operation, at rates now observed.

**This is the one CAC contests**, and only this one. It is a commitment about *historical boundary conditions*, not about operational method, and it is the direct ancestor of the prior [EPI-001](../traceability/claims/EPI-001.yaml) identifies as driving standard age determinations. It is also the assumption whose downstream consequences [PRD-001](../traceability/claims/PRD-001.yaml) and [PRD-002](../traceability/claims/PRD-002.yaml) test — and inflation is the same move made at cosmological scale, deriving the observed state from generic initial data rather than specifying it, which is why PRD-002 names it as the rival.

**Two disciplines attach to this, and dropping either would cost more than the point is worth.**

*The label is retrospective.* "Methodological naturalism" is a twentieth-century coinage — Paul de Vries at a 1983 conference, published as "Naturalism in the Natural Sciences," *Christian Scholar's Review* 15(1), 1986, 388–396, with Edgar Sheffield Brightman using it earlier and independently. No seventeenth- or nineteenth-century practitioner described himself that way, so the claim is about what their method assumed, never about a doctrine they professed.

*The rival account is strong and must be stated at full strength.* The branch was not a philosophical coup imposed on a working science; it was earned by explanatory success. The decisive fact is that diluvial geology was abandoned **by its own leading practitioner, on the evidence**: William Buckland, Britain's most prominent scientific diluvialist, dropped the geological-deluge and biblical-Flood correlation in his own 1836 Bridgewater Treatise, and accepted Agassiz's glacial explanation by 1840. Any programme proposing to reopen the branch point owes an account of why that abandonment was wrong *on the evidence then available*, rather than a diagnosis of the priors that came after it. CAC has not supplied one, and EPI-003 does not pretend to.

**And it cannot be cited as corroboration.** The trajectory from 1830 to the present is an already-known fact. Treating it as confirmation of PRD-001 or PRD-002 would be accommodation wearing a prediction's clothes, which is the precise failure the [ledger](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md) exists to catch, and it is why both predictions are scored forward over future data releases instead.

## Point 7 — the anomaly set, and why it is not in the registry

The set, as stated:

- **Hubble tension.** Early-universe (CMB) and late-universe (supernovae, Cepheids) determinations of the expansion rate disagree at ~5σ: 67.4 versus 73 km/s/Mpc.
- **Low-entropy initial state.** Under naturalism the early universe should be high-entropy chaos; it is observed to be extremely low-entropy, smooth and ordered. Penrose's estimate is 1 in $10^{10^{123}}$.
- **Fine-tuning of the constants.** Cosmological constant to 1 in $10^{120}$; the strong force to ~0.5%; the triple-alpha carbon resonance at 7.65 MeV; the proton/electron mass ratio; gravity versus electromagnetism to 1 in $10^{36}$.
- **The orderliness of quantum mechanics.** The bottom layer of reality is abstract, mathematical and informational, yet precise to 1 in $10^{12}$. The unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics; It from Bit; Logos as information.
- **JWST early mature galaxies.** Massive rotating disk galaxies with dust, metals and billion-solar-mass black holes at $z > 10$–14, where only small proto-galaxies were expected.
- **Preserved soft tissue.** Flexible vessels, osteocytes and collagen sequence in *T. rex* MOR 1125, *Triceratops*, hadrosaur and mosasaur material, which should not survive $>1$ Myr under known kinetics.
- **Polystrate fossils and soft-sediment folding.** Trees vertical through 10–20 ft of strata; mountain-scale folds bent without fracturing, implying rapid burial and deformation before lithification.
- **Lazarus taxa.** Coelacanth, Wollemi pine, Laotian rock rat, Monoplacophora, horseshoe crab, ginkgo, tuatara, nautilus, found alive and largely unchanged after supposed extinctions of tens to hundreds of Myr.
- **The Cambrian explosion.** Nearly all animal phyla appear in a ~10 Myr window with fully formed eyes, limbs and nervous systems; no Precambrian ancestors; no new phyla since.
- **Homochirality.** Life requires 100% left-handed amino acids and right-handed sugars; unguided chemistry produces racemic mixtures that destroy function; there is no selection before replication.

### Why it is stated here and not registered as claims

Registering these as they stand would add ten `role: accommodation` entries, and the [progressiveness ledger](../traceability/generated/progressiveness-report.md) would move from 5 accommodations and 0 predictions to 15 and 0. Every item is an already-known fact being handled after the fact, which is what `role: accommodation` means and what Lakatos counts at zero. The programme would read as measurably *more* degenerating than it does today, and the `honest_status` gate would pass while it happened, because the gate would be working correctly. That is the ledger doing its job rather than obstructing.

### The argument form also does not run yet

Point 7 is a likelihood-ratio argument: $P(E \mid \text{naturalism})$ is low, therefore something else. The other side of that ratio is $P(E \mid H_{\mathrm{CAC}})$, which [OPN-011](../traceability/claims/OPN-011.yaml) records the programme has never specified. Until it does, the inference is the same one point 4 correctly diagnoses as prior-driven, run in the opposite direction.

There is a second problem, independent of the likelihood. **Anomalies are what a live research programme feeds on.** ΛCDM having ten open problems is evidence that it is being worked on, not that it is false, and a programme that treats the existence of surprises as a refutation has adopted a standard under which every science in history is refuted, including this one.

### The form that would run

Not that anomalies exist, but that **the residuals are directionally biased**. Every item on the list points the same way: systems are observed *more* mature, more ordered, more intact, or earlier-formed than gradualist expectation permits. That is a pattern claim, it is nearly a likelihood, and it forbids something real. CAC would be embarrassed by a class of systems found systematically *less* developed than the gradualist prediction, and the claim stakes out a direction on residuals not yet measured.

Stated that way the set becomes a candidate `role: prediction` rather than ten accommodations. The reformulation is registered as the second route at [OPN-011](../traceability/claims/OPN-011.yaml), and it is why the list is preserved here in full rather than dropped.

### The set is not evidentially uniform, and tiering it makes it stronger

- **Conceded by the mainstream as open problems:** the Hubble tension, the low-entropy boundary condition, fine-tuning, homochirality. These carry weight in argument because the rival programme does not dispute the datum.
- **Live mainstream counter-accounts exist and must be engaged before the item is used:** preserved soft tissue (iron-mediated crosslinking and related preservation proposals), polystrate fossils and soft-sediment deformation, and the JWST high-$z$ masses, which have moved substantially as calibration and AGN contamination have been reassessed.
- **Weakest as stated:** the Lazarus taxa. Morphological stasis is compatible with common descent, and the argument reduces to a claim about fossil-record sampling rather than about physics or chemistry. Carrying it at the same strength as the first tier invites a reviewer to refute the weakest item and dismiss the set.

The tiering is a strengthening move rather than a concession. A list defended at uniform strength is defeated at its weakest point.
