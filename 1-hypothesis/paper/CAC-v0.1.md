## Abstract

Modern cosmology has achieved extraordinary predictive success in describing the behavior of the observable universe. Nevertheless, several persistent tensions remain, including the Hubble tension, the nature of dark matter and dark energy, the origin of the universe's exceptionally low-entropy initial state, and the unexpectedly mature structures revealed at high redshift. These are commonly treated as isolated problems within the standard cosmological paradigm.

This paper proposes an alternative research programme: **Creation Actualization Cosmology (CAC)**. Rather than modifying the standard model through additional parameters or entities, CAC begins with a different historical boundary condition. It treats Genesis as a historical account of a discrete creative actualization in which God established a functionally mature cosmos during Creation Week. Ordinary physical laws govern the universe thereafter.

The purpose of this paper is not to claim that CAC has displaced ΛCDM as a quantitative cosmological model. Rather, it argues that CAC constitutes a coherent research programme whose explanatory architecture naturally accommodates several observations currently regarded as cosmological tensions.

## 1. Introduction

Every cosmological model begins with assumptions.

The standard cosmological model assumes that the observable universe developed through a continuous physical history extending approximately 13.8 billion years. Present observations are interpreted by reconstructing this uninterrupted history through the known laws of physics.

Creation Actualization Cosmology begins elsewhere.

It assumes that Genesis provides a historically reliable account of divine creation and that the universe was discretely actualized into operational existence during Creation Week. Consequently, present observations need not encode an uninterrupted natural history. They instead reflect both ordinary physical evolution and the conditions intentionally established at creation.

The distinction is fundamental.

CAC does not seek to repair ΛCDM.

It replaces its historical boundary conditions.

Creation Actualization Cosmology rests upon five axioms.

**Axiom 2.** Creation is an act of divine actualization rather than the culmination of prior natural processes.

**Axiom 5.** Following Creation Week, the universe operates under stable, intelligible physical laws. Miracle characterizes creation itself. Science characterizes providence thereafter.

## 3. Functional Maturity as a Cosmological Principle

Functional maturity has traditionally been applied to biological life.

CAC extends the principle consistently to cosmology.

A functioning Earth requires:

- stable stellar illumination,
- gravitational architecture,
- celestial navigation,
- seasonal cycles,
- and a dynamically stable universe.

A universe created to support life therefore requires cosmological maturity no less than biological maturity.

This proposal differs fundamentally from nineteenth-century "appearance of age" arguments.

CAC does not assert that God fabricated fictitious history.

Instead, it proposes that God created an operational universe whose physical state was sufficient to accomplish its intended purposes immediately.

Operational completeness does not entail deception.

It entails functionality.

## 5. Reinterpreting Cosmological Tensions

CAC proposes that several unresolved cosmological questions arise naturally from its alternative historical boundary conditions.

### Low Initial Entropy

Standard cosmology begins with an extraordinarily ordered universe requiring explanation.

CAC expects precisely such an initial condition.

Intentional creation specifies order before entropy increases under ordinary physical law.

The low-entropy beginning is therefore a design parameter rather than a statistical improbability.

### Cosmological Redshift

Standard cosmology interprets redshift primarily as evidence for continuous expansion extending billions of years.

CAC allows the possibility that much of the observable metric structure was established during Day Four actualization.

Present redshift would therefore reflect the created geometry of spacetime together with subsequent cosmic evolution.

### Hubble Tension

The persistent disagreement between early- and late-universe measurements may indicate that the assumption of a single uninterrupted expansion history is incomplete.

CAC predicts that attempts to reconstruct cosmic history from present observations may produce systematic discrepancies if creation involved a discrete cosmological actualization.

The tension becomes conceptually unsurprising.

### JWST Early Structure

Some early JWST observations suggested unexpectedly mature galaxies at high redshift.

Although many initial claims have since been moderated through improved modeling and additional observations, CAC predicts that apparent maturity at great cosmological distance should not necessarily correspond to billions of years of uninterrupted developmental history.

Structural maturity becomes an expected consequence of creation.

### Dark Matter and Dark Energy

Dark matter and dark energy remain inference categories whose underlying physical nature has not been conclusively identified.

CAC does not deny the gravitational observations.

Instead, it asks whether some inferred dark-sector phenomena may represent attempts to reconstruct continuous history from a universe possessing divinely specified initial conditions.

This remains a hypothesis requiring mathematical development.

- historical Genesis,

- functional maturity,
- stable post-creation physical law.

Its **protective belt** consists of hypotheses concerning:

- cosmological expansion,
- gravitational structure,
- redshift,

Future work should determine whether CAC develops into a progressive research programme by generating quantitative models and novel predictions.

## 9. Falsifiability

CAC should not be insulated from empirical investigation.

The programme gains scientific credibility only insofar as it develops mathematically testable consequences.

Examples include:

- quantitative models of cosmological redshift,
- gravitational dynamics following discrete actualization,
- cosmic microwave background implications,
- large-scale structure formation,
- and observational discriminators distinguishing CAC from ΛCDM.

Until such work is completed, CAC remains a philosophical and theoretical research framework rather than a mature physical theory.

## Position Statement

Creation Actualization Cosmology is not a revision of standard cosmology. It is an alternative cosmological research programme founded upon different historical boundary conditions. It accepts the success of modern physics in describing the present operation of the universe while proposing that the universe's initial state resulted from discrete divine actualization into functional maturity. Its scientific value will ultimately depend not upon philosophical preference, but upon its ability to generate mathematically rigorous models and empirically distinguishable predictions.
